Partner with Jenga

Help small businesses
build a stronger future.

We are exploring collaboration with banks and SACCOs to connect everyday financial planning with accessible savings products. Bring your institution's expertise to the businesses using Jenga.

For SACCOs

Support your members' next milestone.

Work with Jenga to help members understand their savings options and build a consistent money-management routine.

  • Present your savings products and membership requirements clearly.
  • Make interest rates and minimum contributions easier to understand.
  • Explore financial education for members and small businesses.

For banks

Meet businesses where they are.

Explore ways to bring relevant banking products to people already tracking their income, expenses and goals.

  • Introduce business savings products with transparent terms.
  • Help customers understand deposit requirements and interest rates.
  • Explore account integrations with customer permission.

How we could collaborate

Start with product information and financial education, then explore a pilot and account integration together. The scope, customer consent and data-sharing arrangements would be agreed with each institution.
